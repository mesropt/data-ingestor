<objective>
Close SHEET-01's last clause: N selected sheets produce N independent datasets, and the human "exports with one action (an archive of N result sets)" (D-11-10).

Purpose: each member already confirms on its own gate and writes its own `EXPORT_BASE_DIR/{run_id}/` directory (fixed filenames are safe because every member owns its directory). What is missing is the bookkeeping that links a member's `run_id` back to its group, and a route that serves the group as one zip.

The per-member gate is `service.confirm`'s existing `NotReadyError` — the archive adds NO new readiness check and never weakens or aggregates a member's amber gate (D-11-08).

Output: `export/archive.py` (stdlib `zipfile`, sanitized entry names), the `record_run` call in confirm, and `GET /api/export/group/{group_id}/archive`.
</objective>
