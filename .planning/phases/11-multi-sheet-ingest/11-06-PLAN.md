<objective>
Fix `structural_hint.py:56` (D-11-22) — a latent bug that this phase makes load-bearing.

The structural-hint resolve route calls `service.resolve_or_map` WITHOUT `schema=`, `sheet=` or `strictness=`, and never checks `result.date_question`. Four confirmed consequences:
1. **No Python-first prefill** — `resolve_table_mapping`'s `if schema is not None:` branch (`service.py:275`) is skipped, so the D-10-03 crosswalk pass never runs on the hint path and every field goes to Claude, at cost, even when the crosswalk covers all of them.
2. **`escalation` is `None`** (`service.py:343` requires `schema is not None`) — the Review screen's escalation line silently disappears after a structural hint.
3. **`vendor_memory` is never computed** — the vendor field is not pre-filled, and `/api/confirm` **requires** a vendor (`confirm.py:69-80`, 422 otherwise).
4. **`result.date_question` is never checked** — an ambiguous date column behind a structural question never raises the date question: the field stays amber and Confirm 422s with no way out. That is the exact dead-end quick task `260712-qgc` fixed on `/api/upload`, re-created one route over.

Every member of a sheet group re-enters this route with an explicit sheet, so the bug stops being latent. Worse for Phase 11: without a retained `sheet=`, resolving a hint would **re-rank the workbook and parse the wrong sheet**.

Purpose: this must be green before the sheet group is built on top of it (11-07 depends on the retained `sheet` and on this route being correct).
</objective>
