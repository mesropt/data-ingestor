<objective>
Give the Schemas page a backend: an explicit edit surface over a governed Schema's canonical fields and vendor aliases (INGEST-05, D-10-12), and the four presets seeded as Schemas so a fresh sign-in has something to select (INGEST-06, D-10-14).

Purpose: D-07-04's augment-only master-map route is correct and must not change — a machine may only add. But only a human may remove or rewrite, and that needs a path of its own, gated on a verified user, with the removal itself attributed. This plan builds that path *beside* the augment path, never through it.
Output: four new routes, four new service functions, the wire models they need, and preset-as-Schema seeding at startup.

**No date-format or upload work in this plan** — that is Plan 05. **No UI** — that is Plans 06/07.
</objective>
